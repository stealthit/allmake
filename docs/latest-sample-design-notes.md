Visual observations below come from actual GDWEB full-page captures, downloaded and inspected: [Studio JT capture](https://gdweb.co.kr/sub/filedata.asp?str_no=28432&sgbn=1), [Daum capture](https://www.gdweb.co.kr/sub/filedata.asp?str_no=28361&sgbn=1). These are listing captures, not fresh browser screenshots; motion timing was not assessed.

1. **Studio JT: full-width media, small navigation, serif statement.** The opening capture is dominated by a close-cropped blue metallic car render, with sparse white links along the top and a centered serif statement near the image's lower edge. Below it, the page switches to a near-black canvas. Application: use one strong photographic/abstract hero crop with restrained navigation, then change the section's tone decisively. [Capture](https://gdweb.co.kr/sub/filedata.asp?str_no=28432&sgbn=1)

2. **Studio JT: large square work images do the explaining.** Work appears as a tight two-column grid of nearly square, edge-to-edge image tiles, each with a short caption underneath; no padded white card wrappers or paragraph summaries surround the images. Material macro imagery, product closeups and vivid graphic tiles vary the image vocabulary while keeping the grid consistent. Application: use a clean image-led process/product grid for materials, automation, studio or craft samples. [Capture](https://gdweb.co.kr/sub/filedata.asp?str_no=28432&sgbn=1), [live project labels](https://www.studio-jt.co.kr/)

3. **Studio JT: editorial rhythm and a strong ending.** Short left-aligned sans-serif section introductions alternate with a large centered serif agency statement and generous dark gaps. News becomes thin-rule horizontal rows with text left and image right. The footer abruptly turns saturated red and ends in an extremely large, thin STUDIO-JT wordmark. Application: alternate one expressive statement with compact functional sections; give each new sample a distinct footer composition instead of repeating the same card stack. [Capture](https://gdweb.co.kr/sub/filedata.asp?str_no=28432&sgbn=1)

4. **Daum: one continuous visual world with a clear type hierarchy.** A blue planetary horizon on black frames a centered, bold two-line Korean hero; the page gradually moves into an electric-blue background. Small UFO/character illustrations recur between sections and at recruitment, tying the page together. Live markup sets the hero at 40px base, 56px medium and 80px large; body CSS includes 16px/1.6 text. Application: give SaaS/energy a single controlled background palette and strong headline scale; use an original repeated motif only when it serves the sample's identity. [Capture](https://www.gdweb.co.kr/sub/filedata.asp?str_no=28361&sgbn=1), [live site](https://daumcorp.com/), [live stylesheet](https://daumcorp.com/_app/immutable/assets/0.n04KPXJD.css)

5. **Daum: compact services, staggered news.** Services form a regular three-column/two-row grid of softly rounded translucent blue panels with small colorful dimensional icons. News changes to two offset image-led columns, paired with a section title in the left rail; white caption panels keep the news readable against the blue background. Application: use regular grids for comparable service choices and a different, editorial image arrangement for stories so neighboring sections do not repeat the same layout. [Capture](https://www.gdweb.co.kr/sub/filedata.asp?str_no=28361&sgbn=1), [live service/news content](https://daumcorp.com/)

Reuse the structural lessons, not the sites' branding, copyrighted imagery, characters or exact compositions. These two examples demonstrate observable treatments; they do not establish a universal industry trend.
